Drone Mapping for Community Land Assessment

Project Summary:
This pilot project demonstrates how low-altitude drone mapping can support land assessment, planning, and local infrastructure decisions. The objective is to produce accurate, easy-to-understand maps for municipal teams and community stakeholders.

Scope of Work:
- Flight planning for 3 villages and surrounding agricultural parcels
- Ground control points for higher positional consistency
- Orthomosaic and surface model generation
- Mapping of access roads, buildings, and drainage patterns

Methods and Tools:
- Drone survey mission planning
- RTK/GNSS checkpoints
- Photogrammetry processing workflow
- GIS-based feature extraction and map composition

Results:
- High-resolution orthomosaic outputs for each site
- Base map layers for planning discussions
- Clear visual evidence for priority infrastructure zones

Impact:
The workflow helps teams move from assumptions to map-backed decisions, improving transparency and planning quality.
